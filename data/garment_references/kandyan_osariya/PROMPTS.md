## Shared prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Generate exactly ONE photorealistic high-resolution full-length Sri Lankan Kandyan saree / Osariya garment reference: {description}. The crucial feature is the distinctive neriya: a clearly visible outward-folded finely pleated waist frill/fan along the right hip and around the waist, above the wrapped floor-length skirt. Show authentic Sri Lankan Osariya construction, a fitted modest full blouse, controlled shoulder drape/fall, wrapped skirt, structured pleats and the waist frill prominently. Do not substitute an ordinary Indian Nivi saree; do not omit the waist frill; no wide ballroom skirt, no bridal gown, no Western peplum dress. Full front view, complete garment from neckline and shoulder to floor-length hem, centered with margins, three-quarter sleeve or short sleeve as specified, detailed weave and consistent border/print. Photographed on an invisible dress form: absolutely no visible mannequin, person, skin, head, hands, arms, legs or feet. Plain white background, soft even studio light, sharp fabric details, realistic folds, minimal shadow. No jewellery, veil, accessories, hanger, text, logo, watermark, collage or multiple views. Portrait 3:4 composition. Synthetic catalogue garment reference for virtual try-on.
