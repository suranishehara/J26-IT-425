Tool: built-in image generation. Five independent calls. Synthetic product references, not real paired evaluation data.

## Shared prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create ONE photorealistic high-resolution apparel catalogue reference photograph: {description}. Portrait 3:4 composition, exactly one complete dress, front facing, centered and symmetrical, laid flat with subtle realistic fabric shaping. Show the entire neckline, sleeves, waist, skirt and bottom hem with clear margins on every side. Sleeves extend slightly outward, do not overlap the torso. Plain uniform white background, even soft studio lighting, sharp fabric texture and clear print, clean edges, minimal shadow. No person, mannequin, skin, body parts, hanger, accessories, shoes, text, brand marks, watermark, collage or extra views. Standalone garment reference for virtual try-on.
