Fashion chatbot research messages

Participation is voluntary. Please write how you would naturally speak to a fashion assistant. Use your own words, not copied messages or AI-generated text. Include Sri Lankan and/or global clothing topics you know. Do not include names, contact details, private medical information or unnecessary body measurements.

This form runs locally. It does not upload or save answers automatically. After downloading your response file, share it with the researcher through the agreed university collection process. Follow the study information and consent arrangements supplied separately by the researcher.

For follow-ups, include enough context to explain what you are changing, comparing or referring to. If a task is unclear, leave it blank and tell the researcher rather than copying another participant.
